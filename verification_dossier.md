## Methods

**Evidence tiers:** Tier 1A — FDA USPI label claim, registry result matched to a USPI §14 row, or head-to-head Phase 3/4 superiority; Tier 1B — Industry-sponsored registrational Phase 3/4 RCT — primary or multiplicity-controlled key secondary endpoint; Tier 2 — Industry Phase 2 RCT, Phase 3 nominal (non-controlled) secondary, post-hoc / pooled analysis, or (network) meta-analysis; Tier 3 — Academic / investigator-initiated study, observational cohort, case series, or exploratory endpoint

**Validity levels:** L5 — USPI §14 label claim, or Phase 3 primary endpoint positive (p<0.05) in ≥2 trials; L4 — Single Phase 3 primary endpoint, Phase 3 multiplicity-controlled key secondary, or large (N≥300) Phase 4 RCT; L3 — Phase 3 nominal secondary / post-hoc, meta-analysis, or RWE cohort with N>500; L2 — Phase 2 RCT, or academic trial with N 50–500; L1 — Small IIS (N<50), case series, exploratory biomarker, or unclassifiable

Every quantitative claim is drawn from a registry field, USPI section or citation-validated verbatim quote stored in mednik.
